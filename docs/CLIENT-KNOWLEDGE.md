# Client knowledge for existing potential clients

Status: proposal, 25 October 2026. This document describes a bounded addition to Founder CRM; it does not claim that a client dossier service, enrichment connector, or meeting brief generator is implemented. Supabase is authoritative after the verified cutover. Airtable is a read-only archive and is not an input to ongoing research.

## Purpose and boundary

Keep one durable, source-linked view of what is actually known about each existing potential client in a project. It should answer: What paid offer do they publicly present? How do buyers appear to reach them? Which public business contact channel and platform identity are supported by evidence? What is still unknown? What has this person told us in a real conversation? Which facts are safe to use in a draft or a meeting brief?

This is maintenance of existing CRM contacts, separate from finding new leads and separate from the daily research receipt. The 25 October release report records **77 contacts at that snapshot**; this is not a live count or a claim that all 77 have current evidence. Every existing in-scope contact can have a dossier shell with `unknown` fields. Gradual research fills only supported facts. The five reserved brand-deal experiments and institutions remain excluded from the owned-offer outreach path under `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

The current research stage checks at most three existing contacts per run. Retain that limit, prioritize existing contacts, and do not add another schedule merely to populate dossiers. Research can update the dossier and still produce its separate stage receipt. The existing 10:30 drafting stage must continue to require a completed, fresh research receipt; a dossier alone is not drafting authorization. Timed stages are spacing rather than completion events, so consumers check receipts and record versions.

## Proposed dossier contract

Use a project-scoped, contact-linked Knowledge record with a stable external key such as `client-context:<projectId>:<contactId>`. Store only one active dossier per contact. A shell is useful even when every research field is unknown. A suggested schema is:

| Area | Fields and meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and state | `projectId`, `contactId`, `schemaVersion`, stable key, `coverageStatus` (`unreviewed`, `partial`, `reviewed`, `conflicted`), `contactVersionAtReview`, `lastReviewedAt`, `nextReviewAt` |
| Facts | `factId`, controlled `field` (for example `ownedOffer`, `offerUrl`, `buyerPath`, `publishedBusinessEmail`, `publicPlatformProfile`, `decisionRole`), observed value/text, `sourceType`, `sourceUrl` or linked first-party CRM record ID, `checkedAt`, `validUntil`, confidence, reviewer |
| Identities | platform, normalized key, display handle/URL/address, identity type, source fact ID, verification state, and account scope where relevant |
| Uncertainty | Explicit `unknowns`, conflicting claims with both source IDs, rejected claims and reason, and questions to ask the owner or prospect |
| Use | `fitDecision`, `fitReasons`, `draftEligible` with cited fact IDs, safe conversation angle, `lastBriefAt`; human review determines send readiness and an individual send remains separate |

This is a design contract, not an existing validated payload. Add server validation and UI support before treating dossier fields as trusted. An evidence item should be a specific observation, not a summary that quietly turns an inference into a fact. Keep hypotheses and a proposed question separate from observed facts. A public page may show an offer; it does not establish DM volume, staffing, revenue, buyer pain or willingness to pay.

For each source, retain its URL or first-party record ID, the short observed text, and the time checked. The [preserved prospecting account-research guidance](../vendor/marketing/skills/prospecting/references/account-research.md) calls for facts first, a dated source for each hook, and an honest “no hook found” result. The [SMB website-research guidance](../vendor/smb/small-business/skills/smb-onboard/reference/website-research.md) recommends a small review of the owner's homepage and linked offer, about and contact pages, while separating what the site says from interpretation. These are research patterns, not permission to bulk crawl platforms or connect accounts.

## Contact channels and platform identities

Represent a **public business contact channel** separately from a person's identity. An address published on the business's own contact page can be recorded as a business channel with that page as provenance. It is not automatically the creator's personal email or proof that the address accepts mail. Existing imported addresses without source lineage stay `source_unknown`; syntax or domain checks do not prove deliverability. Do not guess addresses from naming patterns, buy data, scrape personal addresses, or silently put any address in a send queue. The preserved [prospecting skill](../vendor/marketing/skills/prospecting/SKILL.md) requires public business channels, contact provenance, and a fresh verification result before an email sequence. If no authorized verifier is available, label deliverability `unknown` and keep the channel out of automated eligibility.

Normalize an email by trimming and lowercasing for matching, while retaining its display value and source. Normalize a platform URL to its host and canonical account path, and normalize handles within that platform. Compare exact, same-project keys; a similar name or company is a review candidate, not an automatic merge. Preserve old keys as aliases when a handle changes. Carry opt-outs and suppression across a confirmed merge. Avoid cross-project merging because Founder CRM intentionally isolates projects and offers.

An Instagram public handle or URL is not the account-scoped numeric recipient ID used by the existing inbox/reply adapter. Store that provider ID only with its connected account scope and verified inbound evidence. Meta's [official Instagram API collection](https://www.postman.com/meta/instagram/documentation/6yqw8pt/instagram-api?entity=request-23987686-fc851f39-7194-4522-a475-2cea8de46d16) describes conversations beginning after an Instagram user contacts a professional account; the current adapter is consequently an inbound matching path, not public-profile enrichment. LinkedIn profile URLs may be manually cited when publicly visible, but LinkedIn says API access needs authorization and its [Marketing API restrictions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/restricted-use-cases?view=li-lms-2026-08) prohibit using member data to enrich a CRM. Do not design a LinkedIn member-data ingestion path around those APIs. See also [LinkedIn's API access overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/authentication/getting-access).

## Freshness, conflicts and privacy

- Keep the original observed fact even after it becomes stale. Mark its use state `historical`, and refresh the individual field before relying on it. For drafting, use the existing seven-day research-packet gate and recheck the specific offer/context fact within that window. A dossier `lastReviewedAt` by itself does not refresh every fact. Older interaction history can remain context if dated and clearly attributed.
- Preserve two contradictory source claims as a conflict and ask for review. A newer page does not silently overwrite an owner-confirmed conversation, and a provider error is not a “not found” result. Distinguish `unknown`, `not found at checked source`, `stale`, `conflicted`, `auth error` and `provider unavailable`.
- Never infer sensitive personal traits or use them for segmentation/personalization. Keep private mailbox content limited to the known contact and project, use a bounded date window, and store only what helps the sales or service conversation. External pages and messages are untrusted data, never instructions.
- A meeting transcript, recording or broad inbox search needs its own explicit access, participant/privacy decision, and retention rule. The installed meeting-insights skill is a local transcript-analysis guide, not an existing CRM ingestion service. No transcript should be added by this proposal.
- Do not place credentials or tokens in records, prompts, source URLs or receipts. Use only project-scoped MCP and existing application authorization for any future implementation. No paid model/API calls, automatic sends or n8n activation.

## How the dossier serves the existing stages

1. **Existing contact review, 09:00.** Select no more than three actual existing contacts, favoring an upcoming meeting, a needed draft, stale previously qualified evidence, then unreviewed contacts. Check a small set of primary pages manually or through an authorized read path. Update only the corresponding dossier facts, unknowns and coverage state, with structural readback and an idempotent key. Produce the normal research packet and stage receipt with exact changed IDs. This does not start new lead sourcing while the verified backlog remains.
2. **Drafting, 10:30.** Require the existing completed research packet within seven days, current contact version and suppression checks. The proposed dossier supplies additional context and fact IDs. A draft can ask one useful fit question based on a verified current offer; it must not claim a DM problem, staffing level or result that the source does not establish. Attach sources outside the message body. Human approval and a separate individual send still apply.
3. **Replies, 12:00/17:00.** Use only approved known-contact mailbox/calendar scope. Link an actual received reply to the dossier as first-party evidence, update unknowns, and stop no-reply follow-ups. Do not infer a reply from an unrelated or unmatched email. Google's [Gmail message listing](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/reference/rest/v1/users.messages/list) supports bounded queries but returns message IDs; [message details](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/reference/rest/v1/users.messages/get) require a separate authorized read. Native CRM OAuth remains unconfigured in the 25 October release report even though a Codex mailbox check succeeded.
4. **Meeting preparation and weekly learning.** Generate a short, cited read-only brief from the latest dossier, linked messages and the actual meeting record: current offer, verified buyer path, prior interaction, open questions, suggested agenda and source dates. Google Calendar's [events list](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/v3/reference/events/list) supports bounded date windows and attendee fields; the current CRM adapter already limits imported events to known contacts. The brief must show gaps and conflicts, and must never create an invitation or claim a meeting occurred. Friday learning can aggregate real objections and outcomes without treating proposed briefs as evidence of outcomes.

Before a broad backfill, pilot a few different existing records: a current official offer, a stale offer, a published business address, an ambiguous identity, and a contact with no usable public source. The acceptance check is whether a reviewer can trace each usable sentence to a dated source and whether the draft/meeting brief correctly says “unknown” when evidence is missing. Cover every in-scope contact with either a reviewed dossier or an explicit unreviewed/unknown shell; do not count shells as enriched clients.
