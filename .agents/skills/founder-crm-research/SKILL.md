Before researching, check the completed research receipt for the current Asia/Kolkata date. A completed logical daily receipt means a catch-up window is a successful quiet no-op; do not advance another three contacts. Skip contacts with evidence checked within seven days unless a real new interaction or upcoming meeting warrants a fresh check. Advance the existing backlog; do not repeatedly check the same three contacts or invent facts to clear unknowns. Every research packet must include the contactVersion verified after its contact update/readback. A packet without that version is ineligible for drafting.

Read `docs/AUTOMATION-STAGES.md`, `docs/SCHEDULES.md`, and `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Read the full canonical skills `vendor/composio/lead-research-assistant/SKILL.md`, `vendor/marketing/skills/prospecting/SKILL.md` and its `references/demand-signals.md`. Apply them to at most three actual existing contacts; do not run drafting, reply handling, publishing or sending. Save a research packet and stage receipt with facts, source URLs, freshness, unknowns, a justified draftEligible decision, and a channel dossier. A score is not proof of need. Supabase is authoritative. Do not query Airtable. No paid APIs, n8n, invented evidence, brand-deal mixing or automated outreach.

The channel dossier is evidence, not permission to contact everywhere. Record an Instagram identity only when the current profile/handle has a traceable source; record a LinkedIn URL only when publicly verified; record an email only when it is published as a business contact on the creator's own site or offer page. Store the source URL, observed date, confidence, and delivery status. Never guess an email, pull an unprovenanced directory address, scrape LinkedIn, or consume paid enrichment credits. A weak but relevant existing CRM identity may produce a review-only draft only when its limitation is explicit and a human profile check is required before any use.

Select only active backlog records. Exclude archived, dead, not-a-fit, opted-out,
do-not-contact, suppressed, institutional, and isolated brand-deal records before
choosing up to three contacts. A legacy `Qualified` label is not current proof of
fit; recheck an owned offer, buyer context, and the source date before making a
draft-eligible decision.
