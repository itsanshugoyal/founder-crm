Skip contacts with evidence checked within seven days unless a real new interaction or upcoming meeting warrants a fresh check. Advance the existing backlog; do not repeatedly check the same three contacts or invent facts to clear unknowns. Every research packet must include the contactVersion verified after its contact update/readback. A packet without that version is ineligible for drafting.

Read `docs/AUTOMATION-STAGES.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Read the full canonical skills `vendor/composio/lead-research-assistant/SKILL.md`, `vendor/marketing/skills/prospecting/SKILL.md` and its `references/demand-signals.md`. Apply them to at most three actual existing contacts; do not run drafting, reply handling, publishing or sending. Save a research packet and stage receipt with facts, source URLs, freshness, unknowns and a justified draftEligible decision. A score is not proof of need. Supabase is authoritative. Do not query Airtable. No paid APIs, n8n, invented evidence, brand-deal mixing or automated outreach.

Select only active backlog records. Exclude archived, dead, not-a-fit, opted-out,
do-not-contact, suppressed, institutional, and isolated brand-deal records before
choosing up to three contacts. A legacy `Qualified` label is not current proof of
fit; recheck an owned offer, buyer context, and the source date before making a
draft-eligible decision.
